How Founders Assign Tasks

1
Go to "Assign Task" in the sidebar — only founders have access via the secret key.
2
Choose who to assign to — select any team member. Both founders can assign to anyone including each other.
3
Pick the field / category — Marketing, Development, Design, SEO, Operations, Social Media, etc.
4
Fill in task details — title, description, priority (High/Medium/Low), and due date.
5
Click "Assign Task" — it appears instantly in All Tasks, the member's profile, and Dashboard.
6
Delete a task (founders only) — click the red ✕ button on any task. A confirmation modal appears before deletion.
7
Mark as Done (everyone) — any member or founder can click "✓ Mark Done". The task card turns green with a Done label. Click "↩ Undo" to revert.
